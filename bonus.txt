Bonus:

Colors toggle in menu (play with or without colors).
Additional Color Features:
- Colors for the special items: Each special character has its own unique color for better visual distinction
- Player color changes to red when they have fast speed (after collecting ^)
- Lives turn into red when only 1 life is left, alerting the player to danger.
- K items and the walls are in light purple color.

-Speed cycles counter displayed after the player catches the ^ item, showing how many cycles of fast speed remain

-Deadlock prevention: When no correct answer digit is available, the game prevents a full board from deadlocking by spawning needed items first. These include answer digits, correction items, score/life items, and opponent-answer deletion items, without replacing existing items.
